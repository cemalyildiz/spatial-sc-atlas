What this is

A catalog of publicly deposited spatial transcriptomics and single-cell RNA-seq datasets, built for picking the right dataset rather than for reading abstracts. Records are harvested directly from NCBI GEO and EBI ArrayExpress, then classified so they can be filtered by how the data was produced and what it is about.

Which fields you can trust

Two kinds of field live on every record, and the difference matters when you cite something.

From the source database, verbatimDerived here, automatically
Accession · title · description · organism · sample count · release date · PubMed ID Modality · platform · imaging vs sequencing · disease category · subtype · tissue · cell count

Derived fields are pattern-matched from each record's title and description. They are good enough to filter and browse with, and they are wrong often enough that you should open the source record — one click from every card — before a dataset goes into an analysis. Records carrying a Paired badge are the ones whose text mentions both a spatial platform and single-cell data; confirm the pairing on the GEO page, since a mention is not a guarantee that both live in the same series.

Modalities

Paired
Spatial and single-cell data referenced in the same record — the most useful kind for deconvolution, label transfer and spot annotation.
Spatial
Spatially resolved data only.
Single-cell
Dissociated single-cell or single-nucleus RNA-seq only. Cancer studies only, to keep the catalog focused.

Subtypes

A record can carry several subtype labels at once — a study can be both LUAD and EGFR-mutant — so the subtype filter matches any one of them rather than a single winner. Histological subtypes (LUAD, LUSC, LCNEC, invasive ductal and lobular, ESCC, ccRCC) and molecular ones (EGFR-mutant, ALK-rearranged, KRAS-mutant, IDH-mutant, HER2-positive, MSI-high, HPV-positive) live in the same list.

Short abbreviations are the danger here, so most are deliberately not matched on their own: ILC means innate lymphoid cell far more often than invasive lobular carcinoma, and IDC is also interdigitating dendritic cell. Those are detected from their full phrase only, which means a study that writes nothing but the abbreviation will carry no subtype — absence of a label is not evidence of absence. Subtypes are only assigned to records that already read as cancer.

Imaging-based vs sequencing-based

Imaging-based methods read a targeted gene panel in situ at single-cell or subcellular resolution — Xenium, MERFISH/MERSCOPE, CosMx, seqFISH, STARmap, in situ sequencing. Sequencing-based methods capture transcripts onto a barcoded surface and sequence them, giving whole-transcriptome coverage at spot, bead or region resolution — Visium and Visium HD, Slide-seq, Stereo-seq, GeoMx DSP, DBiT-seq. The trade-off is coverage against resolution, and it is usually the first thing you need to filter on.

How it stays current

A scheduled GitHub Action re-runs the harvest every week, and opens a pull request when the catalog changes, so nothing enters the site without review. The harvest code is scripts/harvest.py — the classification vocabularies live at the top of that file and are meant to be edited.

Citing the data

This site is an index, not a source. Cite the original study and the accession, not this catalog. Check each record's terms of use before redistributing anything: a few carry controlled access even when the metadata is public.
